How it works

  • Schedule and scores come from the nflverse open dataset. Ratings are Elo built from 2021 onward (K=20, margin-of-victory, 25% regression to the mean each offseason, 48-point home edge).
  • Each game: expected margin = Elo gap ÷ 25, with a normal spread of 13.5 points, so every sim also tracks point differential.
  • Seeding: 4 division winners + 3 wild cards per conference. Tiebreakers are simplified: head-to-head, division record (for division ties), conference record, then point differential, then a coin flip. Strength-of-victory/schedule and common-games steps are not modelled, so razor-thin tie scenarios are approximate.
  • It is a model, not a prediction. Injuries (e.g., a QB change) can be approximated with the strength slider, and the rest of the league can be adjusted by locking results.
